Our Philosophy

Coffee is a conversation with the land

At Umber, we believe exceptional coffee begins long before the grinder. It starts at 1,800 metres above sea level, with farmers who tend their trees like family — because they are. Every cup translates the story of a specific hillside, a particular harvest season, a unique set of hands.

How It's Made

The Craft Behind
Every Cup

01 🌱
Farm Selection

We visit every farm we partner with. No brokers — direct relationships at altitude ensure traceable, ethical sourcing.

02 ☀️
Harvest & Process

Hand-picked cherries processed through washed, natural, or honey methods that define each origin's distinct character.

03 🔥
Small-Batch Roast

Our 12kg roaster lets us profile each origin individually. Every roast is logged, cupped, and refined to perfection.

04 ⚗️
Precision Brew

Grind size, water temp, ratio, timing — all calibrated per bean per day. Great coffee is repeatable craft, not luck.
